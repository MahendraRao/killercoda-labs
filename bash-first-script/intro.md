# Your first Bash script, for real

This is the hands-on partner of the article
[Your First Bash Script, From Zero](https://mahendrarao.com/learn/bash-script-from-zero/).
You do not need to have read it first, but it helps.

## How it works

The terminal on the right is a **real Linux machine** (Ubuntu). You do not install anything.
You type commands, then press **Check** to see if you did it right.

**The kitchen idea.** A Bash script is a **recipe card**. Bash is the **chef** who follows the card.
In four short steps you will:

1. Write a recipe card and let the chef cook it.
2. Stamp the card "approved to cook".
3. Fix a card that has a mistake.
4. Teach the chef to decide: spicy or mild.

If **Check** says something is wrong, read the message. It tells you what it found and how to fix it.
Mistakes are the point, so do not worry about making them.

Press **Start** when you are ready.
