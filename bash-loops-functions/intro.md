# Loops and functions, for real

This is the hands-on partner of the article
[Bash Loops and Functions](https://mahendrarao.com/learn/bash-loops-and-functions/).
You do not need to have read it first, but it helps.

## How it works

The terminal on the right is a **real Linux machine** (Ubuntu). You do not install anything.
Some practice files were just placed in your home folder: a `menu` folder, `orders.txt` and `bill.txt`.

**The kitchen idea.** A **loop** is the chef doing the same job for every order on the rail. A **function** is a recipe card you write once and use many times. In four steps you will:

1. Repeat a job with a `for` loop.
2. Loop over the menu files, including one with a space in its name.
3. Read `orders.txt` one line at a time.
4. Print a priced bill using a function.

If **Check** says something is wrong, read the message. It tells you what it found and how to fix it.

Press **Start** when you are ready.
